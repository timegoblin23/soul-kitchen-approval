What needs a decision

The copy is strong on product truth, but three areas need a decision before anything goes live: Lion’s Mane function claims, the Mushroom Nourish Award, and digestive-health language. Bella’s notes are the source of truth. The claim checks below quote the exact lines that need attention.

!

Bella’s non-negotiables

These three notes override anything in the draft. The first two are launch blockers for the Mushroom route.

01

No Lion’s Mane function claims

You can never claim Lion’s Mane delivers any function. Keep the ingredient name, but remove focus, memory, clarity, cognitive resilience and “works as hard as your supplements”. The corrected figure is 2,000mg.

Critical marketing notes · Note 1
02

Mushroom has no Nourish Award

Do not call Mushroom & Thyme “award-winning” and do not attach the Nourish Awards 2024 & 2025 line to the mushroom flavour. Only use the award on flavours that actually won.

Critical marketing notes · Note 1
03

Never “100% whole vegetables”

Say “packed with up to 72% real veg” and explain the rest: herbs, spices and natural ingredients. Do not say every bowl is 72%, and do not turn “up to” into a flat claim.

Critical marketing notes · Note 3 · Brand book p7

How to use this document

Work through the routes in order. The original copy is always kept; your edits sit on top and are listed in the revision history.

1 · Review

Read the copy and the claim checks. Each route shows its suggested creative and its open flags.

Post a comment, request a change, edit the copy inline, or write a fresh rewrite. Nothing is overwritten without a record.

3 · Decide

Set each route and creative to Approved, Changes needed or Reject, then record your sign-off at the end.

Claim checks

Every check below is tied to a route or creative. “High” means it should be resolved before launch. “Medium” needs substantiation or a wording change. “Low” is a style, evidence or asset note. This is a review tool, not legal advice; final claims still need the brand’s regulatory sign-off.

What already holds up

  • Routes 03 and 04 use the corrected 2,000mg Lion’s Mane figure from Bella’s note.
  • Route 04 keeps the authorised vitamin B12 and vitamin D claims and avoids the mushroom award line.
  • No route names a competitor. The comparisons stay at category level.
  • All five creatives are 4:5 and use the brand book’s palette; the four product creatives use their flavour colours.
  • Route 01 keeps “up to 72% real veg” in the body copy, even though the headline still needs the “up to” qualifier.
  • The document uses Soul Soup Regular for display type and Roboto for body copy, matching the brand book’s digital fallback.

Brand-book guardrails

  • Say “packed with up to 72% real veg”; answer “what’s the rest?” with herbs, spices and natural ingredients.
  • Never name a competitor.
  • Do not use “100% natural” claims.
  • Do not lead with “low” or “no” claims.
  • Do not overuse “instant” or “powdered”.
  • Headlines in Soul Soup; body copy in Roboto for digital.
